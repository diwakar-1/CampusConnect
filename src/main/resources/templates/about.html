About The Platform

CampusConnect @ CGC University Mohali

The centralized hub powering premier collegiate events, hackathons, and symposiums.

Our Mission

At CGC University Mohali, we believe extracurricular innovation, hackathons, cultural fests, and athletic competitions are integral to holistic student leadership. CampusConnect was engineered from the ground up using Java Spring Boot 3 and modern web technologies to streamline event administration, participant registration, venue scheduling, QR-based check-ins, and verified credentialing.

Verified Identities

Role-based controls for both internal CGC scholars and approved external colleges.

Zero-Paper Attendance

Real-time attendance scanning preventing duplicate entry and proxy markings.

Instant Certificates

Automated PDF generation with public cryptographic QR verification.
